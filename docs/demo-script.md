# RECYCLINK 90-Second Hackathon Judge Pitch & Demo Script

**Smart India Hackathon 2026 | Problem Statement: SIH26229**  
*“Kabadiwala Connect – Bringing the Informal Collector into the Formal Recycling Chain”*

---

## 30-Second Problem & Hook
> *"Judges, over 90% of India's electronic waste is collected by informal kabadiwalas. But they face three critical barriers: they don't know the real market price of the gold and copper inside; they have no direct access to authorized formal recyclers; and they resort to burning cables and backyard acid leaching.*
> 
> *Meet **RECYCLINK**: the intelligent informal-to-formal platform that makes every transaction fair, formal, and traceable."*

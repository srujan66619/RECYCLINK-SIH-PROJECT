# RECYCLINK — Phase 8 Impact & Analytics Metric Definitions

This document details the exact mathematical formulas, regulatory sources, and computation methods used across the RECYCLINK **E-Waste Circular Economy Command Center** (SIH26229).

---

## 1. Executive KPIs

### 1.1 Total E-Waste Tracked
- **Definition**: Aggregated weight of all verified electronic scrap lots registered by informal collectors into the digital intake ledger.
- **Data Source**: `e_waste_lots.estimated_weight` (and `final_weight` upon handover).
- **Calculation Formula**:
  $$\text{Total Weight (kg)} = \sum_{i \in \text{Lots}} \text{estimated\_weight}_i$$
  $$\text{Total Tonnes (T)} = \frac{\text{Total Weight (kg)}}{1000}$$
- **Update Frequency**: Real-time upon lot intake submission.

---

### 1.2 Traceable Lots
- **Definition**: Total count of unique electronic scrap batches that have been assigned a cryptographic Circular Trace ID (`RC-2026-XXXXXX`) and dynamic QR code.
- **Data Source**: `e_waste_lots.trace_id`.
- **Calculation Formula**:
  $$\text{Traceable Lots} = \text{COUNT}(\{l \in \text{Lots} \mid l.\text{trace\_id} \text{ IS NOT NULL}\})$$
- **Update Frequency**: Real-time.

---

### 1.3 Completed Transactions
- **Definition**: Commercial lot exchanges between informal collectors and authorized recyclers that have completed verified physical scale handover and digital payment settlement.
- **Data Source**: `transactions.status`.
- **Calculation Formula**:
  $$\text{Completed Transactions} = \text{COUNT}(\{t \in \text{Transactions} \mid t.\text{status} = \text{'COMPLETED'}\})$$
- **Update Frequency**: Real-time upon recipient confirmation.

---

### 1.4 Collector Economic Value
- **Definition**: Gross financial payout disbursed directly into informal collectors' verified UPI IDs or bank accounts.
- **Data Source**: `transactions.total_amount` / `transactions.final_price` for completed transactions.
- **Calculation Formula**:
  $$\text{Collector Value (₹)} = \sum_{t \in \text{CompletedTxns}} t.\text{total\_amount}$$
- **Update Frequency**: Real-time upon transaction completion.

---

### 1.5 Verified Handovers
- **Definition**: Physical custody transfers confirmed through calibrated dual-scale weight reconciliation and digital signature attestation between collector and recycler agent.
- **Data Source**: `handover_records.status`.
- **Calculation Formula**:
  $$\text{Verified Handovers} = \text{COUNT}(\{h \in \text{HandoverRecords} \mid h.\text{status} = \text{'VERIFIED'}\})$$
- **Update Frequency**: Real-time upon handover verification.

---

### 1.6 Active Recyclers
- **Definition**: Authorized formal dismantling, segregation, and smelting facilities with valid Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) registration operating on the platform.
- **Data Source**: `recyclers.authorization_status` and `recyclers.is_active`.
- **Calculation Formula**:
  $$\text{Active Recyclers} = \text{COUNT}(\{r \in \text{Recyclers} \mid r.\text{authorization\_status} \in [\text{'VERIFIED'}, \text{'VERIFIED\_DEMO'}] \land r.\text{is\_active} = \text{True}\})$$
- **Update Frequency**: Real-time.

---

### 1.7 Registered Collectors
- **Definition**: Grassroots informal collectors (kabadiwalas) onboarded with digital identities and participating in formal aggregation.
- **Data Source**: `collectors.is_verified`.
- **Calculation Formula**:
  $$\text{Registered Collectors} = \text{COUNT}(\{c \in \text{Collectors} \mid c.\text{is\_verified} = \text{True}\})$$
- **Update Frequency**: Real-time.

---

### 1.8 AI Guardian Anomalies Flagged
- **Definition**: Unresolved risk alerts generated by the AI Transaction Guardian (predatory underbidding $>40\%$ below CPCB benchmark, scale discrepancies $>25\%$, or repeated uncharacteristic rates).
- **Data Source**: `anomaly_alerts.status`.
- **Calculation Formula**:
  $$\text{Open Anomalies} = \text{COUNT}(\{a \in \text{AnomalyAlerts} \mid a.\text{status} = \text{'OPEN'}\})$$
- **Update Frequency**: Real-time on every offer/handover evaluation.

---

## 2. Governance & Circularity Metrics

### 2.1 Formalization Rate
- **Definition**: The percentage of informal collection lots that successfully complete the custody chain and transition into formal smelting/refining without leakage into informal backyard burning.
- **Calculation Formula**:
  $$\text{Formalization Rate (\%)} = \left( \frac{\text{Completed Traceable Lots}}{\text{Total Traceable Lots Handled}} \right) \times 100$$
- **Regulatory Reference**: Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC) E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022.

---

### 2.2 Toxic Leakage Diverted
- **Definition**: Estimated mass of hazardous contaminants (lead, mercury, cadmium, brominated flame retardants) prevented from open-air burning and crude acid leaching.
- **Calculation Formula**:
  $$\text{Toxic Risk Prevented (kg)} = \text{Total Tracked Weight (kg)} \times 0.18$$
- **Empirical Baseline**: UNEP / CPCB estimates indicate $\approx 18\%$ of aggregate mixed e-waste consists of hazardous fractions requiring controlled containment.

---

### 2.3 Avoided CO₂ Emissions
- **Definition**: Net greenhouse gas reduction achieved by substituting virgin ore mining with recycled secondary raw materials.
- **Calculation Formula**:
  $$\text{Avoided } \text{CO}_2\text{ (kg)} = \text{Total Tracked Weight (kg)} \times 1.44$$
- **Baseline**: International Telecommunication Union (ITU) Circular E-Waste LCA index.

---

### 2.4 Estimated Strategic Metal Recovery Potential
- **Gold ($g$)**: $\text{Weight (kg)} \times 0.035$
- **Copper ($kg$)**: $\text{Weight (kg)} \times 0.12$
- **Silver ($g$)**: $\text{Weight (kg)} \times 0.18$
- **Lithium ($kg$)**: $\text{Weight (kg)} \times 0.015$
- **Recoverable High-Grade Plastics ($kg$)**: $\text{Weight (kg)} \times 0.28$
- *Labeling Constraint*: Displayed with explicit qualifier: `"Estimated Recovery Potential based on average component yields."`

## 3. Commercial & Fairness Indices

### 3.1 CPCB Fair Price Conformance
- **Below Fair**: Bid rate $< 0.95 \times \text{CPCB Benchmark Rate}$
- **Within Fair Range**: $0.95 \times \text{Benchmark} \le \text{Bid Rate} \le 1.05 \times \text{Benchmark}$
- **Good Offer**: Bid rate $> 1.05 \times \text{CPCB Benchmark Rate}$

### 3.2 Trace Hash-Chain Integrity
- **Verification Rule**: For each consecutive event $e_k$ in the lot's audit ledger:
  $$\text{SHA256}(e_{k-1}.\text{hash} + e_k.\text{stage} + e_k.\text{timestamp} + e_k.\text{actor}) \stackrel{?}{=} e_k.\text{hash}$$
- **Integrity Status Indicator**:
  - Valid: `✓ NO TRACE INTEGRITY ISSUES DETECTED`
  - Failure: `⚠ REVIEW REQUIRED`
